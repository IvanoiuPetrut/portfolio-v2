## Overview

Marina Group Adriatic runs three marinas. Berths were booked by phone and tracked in a shared spreadsheet, which meant double bookings in high season and staff glued to the phone.

I designed and built a booking platform that shows live berth availability, takes deposits online and gives harbour staff one schedule for all three sites.

## The challenge

- **Boats are not hotel rooms.** A berth fits a boat only within length, beam and draught limits, and some berths can be split.
- **Seasonal spikes.** Most of a year's bookings arrive in about six weeks.
- **Non-technical staff.** The admin side had to work on a tablet, on a pontoon, in bright sun.

## Approach

Availability is computed from berth dimensions and existing bookings in a single SQL query, so the result is always consistent with the database:

```sql
select b.id, b.code
from berths b
where b.marina_id = $1
  and b.max_length_m >= $2
  and b.max_beam_m   >= $3
  and not exists (
    select 1 from bookings k
    where k.berth_id = b.id
      and k.stay && tstzrange($4, $5)
  );
```

An exclusion constraint on the bookings table makes double bookings impossible, even if two people click at the same moment.

The admin views use large touch targets and a high-contrast theme designed for outdoor use.

## Outcome

In the first season, 68% of bookings were made online without staff involvement. Staff saved around nine hours a week on admin, and there were zero double bookings.
