## Overview

Chrono Switch is a 2D pixel art platform game in which the player controls time, using it to reveal hidden paths.

It won an award at [GameDev.js Jam 2024](https://itch.io/jam/gamedevjs-2024/rate/2668069) and was featured in the [Phaser newsletter](https://phaser.io/news/2024/05/chrono-switch).
