## Overview

Weather App lets you search for a city and see its current weather. You can also check the forecast for the next three days and the hourly forecast for the next 24 hours.

I built it as a front-end developer at Graffino, following industry best practices so it works well on any device and screen size.

## What you can do

- Search for a city, with autocomplete
- See the current weather, the next three days and the next 24 hours
- Use your location to get local weather straight away
- Save favourite cities, kept across page reloads
- Pick your units, also kept across reloads

It is built with Vue 3 and the Options API, Vuex for state, Axios for the API calls and SASS for styling.
